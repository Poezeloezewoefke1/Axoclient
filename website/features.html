Features

Built for players who care about the details

A balanced mix of PvP, performance, and quality of life — plus a launcher that does the boring parts for you.

In-game modules

Every feature is a toggleable module with a movable HUD position. Open the menu in-game with Right Shift.

🎯

CPS Counter PvP

Live clicks-per-second for both mouse buttons with a one-second rolling window.

⌨️

Keystrokes PvP

WASD and mouse buttons rendered as clean, responsive on-screen keys.

📈

FPS Display HUD

A crisp frame-rate readout you can place anywhere on screen.

🧭

Coordinates HUD

Your position and facing direction, always visible, always accurate.

💡

Fullbright QoL

See in caves and dark corners without touching your brightness slider — restored exactly on toggle-off.

🔍

Zoom QoL

Hold to zoom with a smooth transition. Perfect for spotting distant targets or builds.

👀

Freelook QoL

Hold a key to look around while you keep running the same way — handy for watching behind you mid-chase.

🚀

Sodium Perf

Bundled and version-matched: a modern rendering engine for dramatically higher FPS.

🧵

Lithium Perf

Bundled game-logic optimizations for smoother ticks and less stutter.

🎛️

HUD Editor QoL

Move any HUD element to nine anchor points and nudge it pixel-by-pixel. Your layout persists.

Combat

Awareness, not advantage — everything here reads information the game already shows you.

🎯

Target HUD PvP

Who you're looking at, their health, and a health bar that drains as you fight.

⏱️

Attack cooldown PvP

A charge bar for your weapon so you hit at full damage instead of spamming.

💥

Damage numbers PvP

Floating numbers for damage dealt, so you can see exactly what a hit was worth.

🔗

Combo counter PvP

Counts consecutive hits and resets when the chain breaks.

📶

Ping & spike warning HUD

Your live latency, plus a warning the moment it jumps — so you know it was lag, not you.

✚

Custom crosshair HUD

Dot, cross or circle in your accent colour, sized how you like it.

Information at a glance

Over forty modules. Turn on the ones you want, put them where you want them.

❤️

Health, hunger & XP HUD

Exact numbers instead of counting hearts, plus levels and progress to the next one.

🛡️

Armour & durability HUD

Every piece with its remaining durability, and a warning before something breaks.

🧪

Potion effects HUD

What's active and how long is left, in plain text.

🌍

Biome, direction & compass HUD

Where you are, which way you're facing, and a compass strip across the top.

⚰️

Death coords HUD

Remembers where you died so your stuff is findable.

📦

Pickup log HUD

A short list of what you just picked up, so a busy floor doesn't hide the good drop.

🖥️

Server info & TPS HUD

Which server you're on and how well it's keeping up.

🕐

Clock, day & session time HUD

Real-world time, in-game day number, and how long you've been playing.

📋

Copy coords QoL

One key copies your position to the clipboard, ready to paste into chat.

💬

Chat tools QoL

Timestamps on every line, duplicate spam hidden, and your sent messages kept across servers and restarts.

🎚️

Tune it in-game QoL

Crosshair size, zoom strength, trail density — adjust them right in the menu. No config files.

Cosmetics

Personalise how you look — capes and trails render locally in your game, and you can change your real skin from the launcher.

🧥

Ten capes Cosmetic

Blue, red, purple, black, green, white, gold, sunset, ocean and carbon — all bundled, no external server needed.

🎨

Your own cape Cosmetic

Drop a 64×32 PNG into your game folder and wear it. Any image you like.

🎆

Twenty particle trails Cosmetic

Flame, soul, smoke, portal, cherry, end rod and more — trails that follow you and auras that surround you.

😇

Halo & wings Cosmetic

A turning ring above your head and swept wings behind your shoulders, both in your accent colour.

🖼️

Skin changer Launcher

Preview your current skin and upload a new one (classic or slim) straight from the launcher — it updates your Microsoft account.

📚

Skin library Launcher

Save your favourite skins in the launcher and swap between them in one click.

Cosmetics render on your own screen only. Showing them to other players would need a server to host the textures — Axo deliberately doesn't run one.

Custom interface

A flat, modern in-game overlay — open it with Right Shift. Everything you change is saved to your config automatically.

🖼️

Custom main menu

Axo artwork replaces the vanilla panorama, with a branded hub screen one click away.

🪟

ClickGUI

Draggable, per-category panels for every module — drop shadows, rounded corners, soft gradients. Left-click to toggle; positions persist between sessions.

🎨

Themes & accents

Pick from eight accent colours and switch between polished dark and light themes on the fly.

🔎

Live module search

Start typing to filter every module instantly — no scrolling through categories.

⌨️

Custom keybinds

Right-click any module to bind a toggle key; the hotkey shows right on the module row.

🔔

Notifications

Clean animated toasts confirm every toggle so you always know what changed.

🎛️

HUD editor

Drag any HUD element with magnetic edge-snapping, or nudge it pixel-by-pixel with the arrow keys.

Launcher

A modern desktop launcher that manages everything so you never open a mod folder.

🔐

Microsoft login

Official OAuth sign-in with an encrypted, persistent session and silent refresh on startup.

👥

Multiple accounts

Add several Microsoft accounts and switch between them instantly — no re-entering passwords.

🖼️

Skin changer

See your current skin rendered in 3D and upload a new one, classic or slim, in a couple of clicks.

☕

Automatic Java

Provisions the exact Java runtime each version needs — no manual installs.

✅

Verified installs

Every download is hash-checked; corrupted files are re-fetched automatically.

🔄

Auto-updates

The launcher updates itself from GitHub Releases — repair re-verifies your install in one click.

📊

Live progress

Per-stage progress for Java, mods, and game files while it works.

🌙

Modern UI

A dark, grid-lit interface with a big 3D character render, a one-press LAUNCH button, and a news panel.

🧩

Mod manager

Add your own mods, turn them on and off, and remove them — repair never touches them.

📸

Screenshot browser

Every screenshot you take, in the launcher — view, reveal in Explorer, or delete.

🧭

Profiles

Save memory, speed preset and channel under a name, then switch setups in one click.

🌐

Saved servers

Keep your favourite addresses as chips and launch straight into one — no menus, no retyping.

🩹

Crash helper

If the game crashes, the launcher reads the report and explains it in plain English — often naming the exact feature at fault.

🎚️

Memory advice

Recommends how much RAM to give the game based on your actual PC, and one-click speed presets.

💬

Discord status

Optionally shows what you're playing on your Discord profile. Off with one switch.
