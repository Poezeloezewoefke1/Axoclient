Why Axo

Everything you need, nothing you don't

A focused feature set that respects fair play — the same line the big clients draw.

⚔️

Balanced PvP

CPS counter, keystrokes, and crisp combat info — competitive awareness without crossing into cheating. Allowed on servers, by design.

⚡

Performance built in

Ships with Sodium and Lithium tuned per Minecraft version and installed for you. Higher FPS, smoother frames, zero mod-folder wrangling.

✨

Over forty modules

Fullbright, zoom, armour and potion readouts, a target HUD, a compass — turn on what you want, put it where you want it.

🧥

Capes & skins

Ten built-in capes, your own cape from any PNG, twenty particle trails, and a skin changer built into the launcher.

👥

Multiple accounts

Sign in with more than one Microsoft account and switch between them in a click — no re-typing passwords.

🔄

Always up to date

The launcher updates itself automatically, so you're always on the latest build without reinstalling.

How it works

Playing takes three steps

Download & install

Grab the Windows installer and run it. No Java setup, no manual mods — the launcher handles everything.

Sign in with Microsoft

Log in with your normal Minecraft account. Your session is encrypted and stays signed in.

Press play

Axo provisions Java, Fabric, and the mods, verifies every file, and launches the game with Axo running.
